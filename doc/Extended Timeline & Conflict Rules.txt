Extended Timeline & Conflict Rules

An existing event of wildfire on date T locks down its municipality for a 29-day total footprint (assuming the event itself is 1 day):
29=T-14 to T+14

The Golden Rule for Placement:
For an NO-event to happen on date T_alt in Municipality M, two conditions must be met:
1. Zero Overlap (Location Availability): 
The entire 29-day footprint of the new event [T_alt-14, T_alt+14] must have NO overlapping days with the 29-day footprint of any existing wildfire event in Municipality M. 
(Mathematically, two events in the same municipality must be at least 29 days apart: !T_alt-T! >= 29.

2. Station Supply Coverage (Resource Availability): 
During the new event's 14-day preparation window  [T_alt-14, T_alt-1] , there must be at least one active station available (matching Time + Location rules) to deliver the 10 daily parameters.

To compute this, we generate a master calendar of all possible (Municipality, Candidate_Date/control dates) pairs, eliminate blocked dates, and validate station coverage.
1. Create a Municipality-Date Grid:
List every unique Municipality paired with every calendar date across target timeframe.
2. Mark Blocked Days (Existing Footprints):
For every existing event on date T, mark all dates from T - 14 to T + 14 as Blocked for that specific municipality.
3. Find 29-Day Contiguous Free Windows:
A candidate date T_alt is only valid if none of the days in its required footprint [T_alt-14, T_alt+14] are marked as Blocked.
	• Shortcut: Simply check the distance to the nearest existing wildfire event in that municipality. 
    If the previous event ended at least 29 days ago AND the next event is at least 29 days away, T_alt is spatially and temporally free.
4. Validate the 14-Day Prep Supply:
Take surviving T_alt dates, expand their 14-day preparation windows [T_alt-14, T_alt-1], and join them to the Stations table using your original rules (Prep_Date BETWEEN Station.Start_Date AND Station.End_Date + Location Match).
	• If all 14 preparation days have at least 1 active station, T_alt is a confirmed valid slot.
